Doorstep is a three-minute ritual for the end of the workday.

How it works

  • Dump what's in your head.
  • Take 3 tomorrow or leave them on the doorstep.
  • Pick them up when you leave in the morning.
  • Hold to close. Work stays outside.

Restore purchases

New phone, or reinstalled the app? Open Doorstep, go to the paywall, and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

To manage or cancel your subscription, open iOS Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
